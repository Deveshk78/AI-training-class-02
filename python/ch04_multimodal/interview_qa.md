# Chapter 4 Interview Q&A

## 1. What is multimodal AI?
Multimodal AI combines different input types such as text, image, audio, and video into a single reasoning pipeline.

## 2. Why is multimodal AI useful for RAG?
It lets the system ingest content from meetings, screenshots, phone calls, and recorded demos and turn them into searchable or summarizable evidence.

## 3. What is a typical image pipeline?
OCR, captioning, object detection, and question answering.

## 4. What is a typical audio pipeline?
Transcription, speaker diarization, summarization, and sentiment or action extraction.

## 5. What is a typical video pipeline?
Frame extraction, captioning, scene analysis, and summarization of the content into actionable notes.
